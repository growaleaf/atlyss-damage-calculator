ATLYSS — Weapon & Skill Damage Projection Calculator

Stand at the enchanter with a weapon or skill tooltip open in ATLYSS, type in the numbers it shows you, and see the projected damage range before you spend your next stat point. Built from the game's own wiki-documented scaling formulas — nothing here is a recommendation about which stat to pick, only the arithmetic. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Weapon damage

Enter the weapon's base damage floor and ceiling from its tooltip, pick which stat governs it, and type the stat total you're considering.

Source: Game Mechanics page, Damage Scaling table Damage Floor = base damage floor + (stat × 0.16). Damage Ceiling = base damage ceiling + (stat × 0.24). Scaling is linear with no diminishing returns; results are rounded down to the nearest whole number, same as the game. The optional cap field lets you enter the weapon's own documented max damage range so the projection never reads past it — no weapon's cap number is built into this page, since ATLYSS is active early access and those numbers change with patches.

Skill damage

Enter the skill's base damage and its power stat from the tooltip. For a Vitality-scaling skill such as Siphon, enter your current max HP as the power value instead of a stat total.

Source: Skill page — marked an incomplete/stub page as of 2026-10-21 Minimum damage = base damage + (power × 0.36). Maximum damage = minimum damage + 3. This page is the wiki's own description and is self-labeled unfinished, so treat this section with less confidence than the weapon-damage section above and verify it against your current patch. (The wiki's separate Game Mechanics table also lists a "Skills" row using ×0.36 for both ends instead of a flat +3 — the two live pages disagree; see Sources below for both quotes. This calculator uses the Skill page's own explicit wording.)

Sources & last verified date

Both wiki pages fetched directly 2026-10-21. This page gives no build, class, or weapon ranking anywhere — only the computed damage numbers for what you type in.

  1. Weapon damage scaling — atlyss.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_Mechanics, Damage Scaling section. Quoted directly: "Weapon damage scales with a particular stat depending on the weapon type," "Damage scaling is linear with no diminishing returns," "A weapon's damage cannot be increased past the max damage range for that weapon," and "any decimal values are always rounded down to the nearest integer to give you your final value." The page's table gives, for Strength/Dexterity/Mind weapon types: Damage Floor = Base Damage Floor + (stat × 0.16), Damage Ceiling = Base Damage Ceiling + (stat × 0.24). Strength weapons (Melee, Heavy Melee, Polearm) use Attack Power; Dexterity weapons (Katar, Ranged) use Dex Power; Mind weapons (Scepter, Bell) use Magic Power. This page is a stable mechanics reference, not a stub, so it leads this product.
  2. Skill damage — atlyss.wiki.gg/wiki/Skill. Quoted directly: "The minimum damage of skills can be calculated by taking 36% of the respective skill's power" and "adding it to the base damage of the skill," then "The maximum damage of skills can be calculated by adding 3 to the minimum damage." Also: "Skills that scale off of Vitality, such as Siphon leech, use your maximum health as their 'power'." The page carries its own notice: "This page is a stub. You can help the ATLYSS Wiki by expanding it." — reproduced here as the reason this section is labeled lower-confidence.
  3. Conflicting table on the Game Mechanics page: that same page's Damage Scaling table also lists a "Skills" row giving Damage Floor = Base Damage Floor + (Power × 0.36) and Damage Ceiling = Base Damage Ceiling + (Power × 0.36) — i.e. the same 0.36 coefficient for both ends, which does not match the Skill page's flat "+3" maximum. The two pages were fetched on the same date and disagree with each other; this calculator follows the Skill page's own explicit wording for skill damage, since that page is the one the wiki specifically dedicates to skill mechanics. Re-verify both pages against your current patch before relying on either formula for a decision that matters.
  4. Weapon Mastery — explicitly NOT modeled: the Game Mechanics page also states that Weapon Mastery multiplies a weapon's base damage by 1.4 under a separate table, and that skill damage is unaffected by Mastery. This calculator does not include a Mastery toggle — it projects from whatever base damage floor/ceiling you type in, so if your weapon tooltip already reflects Mastery, the numbers you enter already include it.
  5. Level cap, patch-specific constants — explicitly NOT hardcoded: this lane's own prior research found atlyss.wiki.gg version pages disagreeing on the level cap (40 on one page, 32 on a page citing a later patch). ATLYSS is active early access. No stat total, base damage value, or weapon cap is pre-filled anywhere on this page for that reason — every field starts at 0 and is read from your own current tooltip.
